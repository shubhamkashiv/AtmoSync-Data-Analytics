# Shipment Risk Level Analysis – Findings

## 1. Objective
The objective of this analysis is to examine the distribution of risk levels across six shipments (S1–S6) and identify missing risk records.

## 2. Key Findings
- Risk level 2 has the highest number of records across all six shipments.
- Shipment S2 contains the highest number of risk level 2 records (2,284).
- Risk level 1 has comparatively fewer records across all shipments.
- Shipment S1 has the highest number of missing risk labels (1,031), followed by S2 (144).
- Shipments S3, S4, S5 and S6 have only 5 missing risk labels each.

## 3. Visualization
The shipment-wise risk level distribution is represented using a grouped bar chart.

**File:** `shipment_risk_distribution.png`

## 4. Business Insights
The distribution shows differences in risk-label patterns across shipments. Shipment S1 and S2 also require additional attention because they contain more missing risk records.

The exact meaning of risk levels 0, 1 and 2 must be verified from the dataset documentation before assigning severity or spoilage interpretations.

## 5. Conclusion
The analysis identifies variations in risk-level distribution and missing records across shipments. Further investigation is required to understand the meaning of risk categories and their relationship with temperature fluctuations and shipment spoilage.
