# Temperature vs Risk Level Analysis

## 1. Objective
To analyze the relationship between shipment temperature and recorded risk levels.

## 2. Key Findings
- Risk Level 0.0 contains 2,419 records with an average temperature of 2.12°C.
- Risk Level 1.0 contains 104 records with an average temperature of 2.13°C.
- Risk Level 2.0 contains 10,527 records with an average temperature of 17.97°C.
- Risk Level 2.0 shows a wider temperature range, from -0.12°C to 33.87°C.

## 4. Business Insights
The analysis shows a noticeable difference in average temperature across the recorded risk levels. Risk Level 2.0 has a considerably higher average temperature than Levels 0.0 and 1.0.

Further investigation is required to understand the exact meaning of each risk level and determine whether temperature is a contributing factor.

## 5. Conclusion
The analysis identifies a temperature variation pattern across risk categories. Additional validation using dataset documentation and other shipment parameters is required before drawing operational conclusions.
