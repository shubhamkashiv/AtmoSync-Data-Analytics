# Shipment-wise Temperature Analysis

## 1. Objective
The objective of this analysis is to compare the average temperature across six shipments using the W60_T_mean feature.

## 2. Key Findings
- Shipment S2 recorded the highest average temperature of 19.04°C.
- Shipment S5 recorded the lowest average temperature of 10.82°C.
- Shipment S1 recorded the highest maximum temperature of 33.87°C.
- Shipment S4 showed the highest temperature variation with a standard deviation of 10.12.

## 3. Visualization
A bar chart was created to compare the average temperature of all six shipments.

## 4. Business Insights
Shipment S2 has a higher average temperature compared to the other shipments. Shipment S1 recorded the highest maximum temperature, while S4 showed greater temperature variability. These shipments can be investigated further using temperature history and risk indicators.

## 5. Conclusion
The analysis highlights temperature differences across shipments. Further investigation of temperature fluctuations and risk levels is required before drawing conclusions about shipment spoilage.
